Real Costs

What tool disorganization actually costs.

In lean environments, a missing tool stops the line — unaccounted tools are non-value-added waste. But the steepest cost is the one that never shows on a report: a disordered shop teaches the team to stop caring.

Downtime

Every tool search is non-value-added time. In a 5S environment, an unaccounted tool means line stoppage until it's found or replaced.

Audit gaps

Unorganized storage means manual inventory before every internal or compliance audit. A shadow board makes the inventory continuous and visual — no prep required.

Attrition

Walked-off and unaccountable tools don't appear on any single report. Point-of-use visual accountability makes attrition visible at the drawer level.

The Bigger Cost
Eroded care

When a workspace isn't cared for, the care goes out of the work. People rush, tools get damaged, quality slips, and accountability disappears. A visual system makes "cared for" the default — not a daily fight.

Aviation & Aerospace
$13B

Core Material

XLPE Closed-Cell Foam

Cross-linked polyethylene is the gold standard for professional tool organization. Unlike open-cell foam, it doesn't absorb moisture, resists chemicals, and won't compress under heavy tools over time.

  • Chemical & oil resistant
  • High-density — won't compress over time
  • Available in multiple colors for color-coding
