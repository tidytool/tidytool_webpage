The Process

How it works

From request to drop-in organizers — three steps, and we handle the work in between.

Schedule onsite scan service

Request a scan through the form and we'll set a time with your point of contact. We scan your tools on site — no templates, no measuring, no effort on your end.

Review design through online portal

Your scans become a precise digital layout, every tool mapped to its place. If you opt for labels, you approve them inside your customer portal before we cut.

Install organizers

Your CNC laser-cut inserts arrive ready to go. Drop them in the drawer and you're organized — no trimming, no setup.

Why It Works

Disorganization has a cost. This is the fix.

Every tool search is non-value-added time — and the deeper cost is a shop that stops caring. Shadow boards hold the standard shift after shift, without supervision.

See It Missing

Two-Tone Visibility

A bright base layer under dark foam makes every empty pocket obvious. Visual inventory in seconds, every shift — no manual counting before an audit.

Mistake-Proofing

Poka-Yoke Fit

Every pocket is CNC laser-cut for one tool, one way — a wrong tool won't seat. The standard lives in the foam, not on a laminate above the box.

Built to Last

Premium XLPE Foam

Closed-cell, waterproof, chemical- and oil-resistant, and won't compress under heavy tools. Lifetime warranty on every physical product.

Accountability

Digital Tool Tracking

Every organizer pairs with digital tracking — what's checked out, what's missing, who's accountable. Walked-off tools surface before they're gone for good.
