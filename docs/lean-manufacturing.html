The Challenge

Sustain is the hardest S to keep.

Every kaizen event looks good on audit day. Two weeks later, the standard slips. Tool control decays not because workers are careless — but because nothing enforces it between shifts.

5S · Sustain

Standards Slip Without Physical Enforcement

A laminated diagram above the toolbox is easy to ignore. A foam insert that only accepts the right tool in the right spot is not. Visual management works when it's built into the workspace, not posted above it.

Tool Accountability

Missing Tools Stop the Line

Every tool search is non-value-added time. In a takt-driven environment, a missing socket or wrench delays the next station. Point-of-use accountability closes that gap at the drawer — before it becomes a line stoppage.

Audit Readiness

Continuous Inventory, Zero Prep

With shadow-board foam, the tool inventory is visible at all times. No pre-audit counting, no manual checklists — any absent tool is self-announcing. When the 5S audit walks the floor, the standard is already showing.

Visual Management · Poka-Yoke

An empty pocket announces itself.

Two-tone XLPE foam turns every tool drawer into a visual management system. A missing tool is visible at a glance — no count, no checklist required.

Set-in-Order · Sustain

Built-In Visual Control

The contrast layer beneath the cut surface shows through every empty pocket. At a shift change, a 5S audit, or a mid-task interruption, accountability is instant — a visual scan, not a count.

The standard doesn't decay because it isn't a standard anyone has to remember. It's cut into the foam.

Mistake-Proofing

One Tool. One Pocket. One Way.

Each pocket is cut to the exact profile of one tool. A wrong tool won't seat fully. A right tool placed in the wrong pocket won't fit at all. Poka-yoke is built into the insert — not posted on a sign above the toolbox.

That's the difference between a reference standard and an enforced one.

Materials

XLPE foam built for real work environments.

Core Material

Cross-Linked Polyethylene (XLPE)

XLPE is a closed-cell foam — it does not absorb water, coolant, cutting oil, or shop chemicals. Unlike open-cell kaizen foam, it won't compress under heavy tools over time. The pocket profile holds its shape for years of daily production use.

  • Waterproof and chemical-resistant
  • Non-compressing under heavy or sharp tools
  • Soft enough to protect tool coatings and edges
  • Multiple color combinations available for color-coded 5S programs
Two-Tone Construction

Shadow Effect at Every Pocket

Two layers of contrasting foam are bonded before cutting. When a tool is removed, the lower layer shows through the pocket in a high-contrast color — making absences visible from across the aisle, not just up close.

This is the same principle behind industrial shadow boards used in aerospace and FOD-controlled environments: physical visual management, not paper-based accountability.

Insert Options

Three tiers to match your program's standard.

All tiers use the same AI-designed layout and CNC-cut process. The difference is in surface finish, labeling depth, and top-layer durability.

Professional Tier

Carbon-Fiber Acrylic Top Layer

A rigid carbon-fiber-look acrylic top layer is CNC-engraved with tool names, part numbers, or department labels. Engraving is permanent — not a label that peels after six months of shop use. Right for long-term programs where the standard needs to hold through staff turnover.

Standard Tier

Two-Tone Foam with Engraving

The same two-tone XLPE construction with engraved text in the foam surface — tool name, slot number, or custom label. Durable through daily shop use. A strong default for most lean manufacturing and kaizen programs.

Minimum Tier

Two-Tone Foam, No Engraving

Two-tone XLPE with precision-cut pockets and no text engraving. Visual accountability is fully functional — the contrast layer announces every missing tool. Right for pilot programs or applications where permanent labeling isn't required.
