Core Material

XLPE Closed-Cell Foam

Cross-linked polyethylene is the gold standard for professional tool organization. Unlike open-cell foam, it doesn't absorb moisture, resists chemicals, and won't compress under heavy tools over time.

  • Waterproof & moisture-resistant
  • Chemical & oil resistant
  • High-density — won't compress over time
  • Available in multiple colors for color-coding
  • Soft enough to protect tool edges and coatings
Cutting Technology
