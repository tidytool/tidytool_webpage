# Google Business Profile Setup Checklist

**Owner action required — do not delegate this to an AI tool.**
GBP requires identity verification and is tied to your legal business entity.

---

## Before You Start

- [ ] Decide which legal entity name to register under. This should be your registered business
      name — not "TidyTool" unless that's the legal entity. The profile transfers through a rename
      far better than backlinks do, so register under whatever name is on your EIN / business registration.
- [ ] Have your business phone number and website URL ready (`thetidytool.com`).
- [ ] Prepare 5–10 install photos (JPG/PNG, at least 720px wide). Real drawers, real tools seated.

---

## Step 1 — Create the Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and sign in with the Google account
   you want to own this profile long-term (use a business Gmail, not a personal one).
2. Click **Add your business**.
3. Enter your business name (legal entity name).
4. Select the primary category: **Manufacturer**
   - Add secondary category: **Industrial Equipment Supplier**
5. When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?", select **No** — this is a
   service-area business. Do not show a storefront address.
6. Set your service area: add **Logan, UT**, **Cache Valley**, and **Box Elder County, UT**.
   Google will expand by radius — you can adjust after verification.
7. Enter your contact information:
   - Phone: `(435) 999-4824`
   - Website: `https://thetidytool.com`
8. Select your business hours (or mark as "By appointment only" if that's accurate).
9. Click **Finish** and proceed to verification.

---

## Step 2 — Verification

Google will offer one or more verification methods. Most common for a new service-area business:

- **Postcard by mail** — a postcard with a code mails to your address (5–14 days). Enter the
  code in your GBP dashboard when it arrives.
- **Phone or video call** — may be offered as an alternative; follow the on-screen prompts.

Do not make major edits to the profile while verification is pending — it can reset the process.

---

## Step 3 — Complete the Profile (after verification)

Work through each section in the GBP dashboard:

### Business Description
Keep it under 750 characters. Suggested draft:

> TidyTool designs and manufactures custom AI-designed, CNC-cut two-tone foam tool organizers
> and shadow boards for lean manufacturing facilities, technical schools, and aviation maintenance.
> Made in Logan, Utah. We serve Cache Valley, Box Elder County, and Northern Utah.

### Services
Add individual services (GBP has a structured services field):
- Custom foam tool inserts
- Shadow boards (5S tool control)
- CNC-cut foam organizers
- On-site design and tool inventory consultation
- Two-tone XLPE foam inserts
- Foam inserts for tool drawers

### Photos
Upload in these categories:
- **Exterior / At work**: shop or CNC machine photos if available
- **Products**: install photos showing tools seated in foam
  - `shadow-board-drawer-tools-seated.jpeg`
  - `foam-inserts-cnc-cut-layout.jpeg`
  - `two-tone-xlpe-foam-detail.jpeg`
  - Add any before/after pairs as they become available
- Aim for 5–10 photos minimum; more is better for first-page Local Pack ranking

### Attributes
Enable any that apply:
- "Identifies as veteran-owned" (if applicable)
- "Identifies as locally owned"
- Service options: "Online estimates" (since you take quote requests via the site)

Within the first week of going live, publish a GBP post to signal the profile is active.

Suggested first post (keep under 300 words):

> Custom foam tool organizers, designed and cut in Logan, Utah. We build two-tone XLPE shadow
> boards for 5S programs, technical schools, and any shop where tool accountability matters.
> If you're running a kaizen event or need audit-ready tool control at your facility, reach
> out for a free quote — thetidytool.com

Add a button: **Learn more** → `https://thetidytool.com`

## Ongoing (monthly)

- [ ] Respond to any reviews within 48 hours (positive and negative).
- [ ] Add a new photo after each install.
- [ ] Publish a post after significant installs or program updates (1–2 per month is enough).
- [ ] Monitor the "Questions & Answers" section — seed it with 1–2 common questions if none
      appear organically.

---

## What NOT to do right now (brand-hedge rules)

- Do not submit the GBP listing to third-party directories (Yelp, Angi, BBB, etc.) — those
  citations compound under the business name and are harder to transfer through a rename.
- Do not run Google Ads tied to this profile until the rebrand decision is made (~September 2026).
- Do not add a physical storefront address — keep this as a service-area-only listing.
