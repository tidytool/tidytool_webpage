## For the testimonial quote
You want one or two punchy, quotable lines. Ask, then shut up and let him talk — write down his exact words.

1. In one or two sentences, how would you describe what the TidyTool boards do for your program?
2. What's the difference you'd point to — before vs. after having them?
3. If another instructor asked you "should I get these?", what would you tell them?
4. What surprised you, or what do you like most about them?

> Listen for a single clean sentence. If he says something great, repeat it back: "Can I quote you saying exactly that?"
